# Acrylic Panel — Coating Degradation

The vacuum-deposited metallic coating on the acrylic mirror panel degrades over time — clouding, hazing, or developing spots that reduce the infinity mirror effect.

---

## Symptoms

- The mirror surface looks hazy or cloudy compared to when it was new.
- Small spots or patches where the reflective coating has worn away, creating dark areas in the infinity tunnel.
- The infinity depth effect is weaker — fewer visible "layers" of reflection.
- Discoloration or yellowing of the reflective surface.

## Likely Causes

- **UV exposure** — direct sunlight on the mirror face degrades the metallic coating over years. UV also yellows the acrylic substrate.
- **Chemical exposure** — cleaning with ammonia-based products (Windex), alcohol, or abrasive cleaners strips the coating. Standard glass cleaner is the enemy here.
- **Moisture** — extended exposure to high humidity (bathroom placement without ventilation) can cause the coating to delaminate at the edges.
- **Abrasion** — wiping with rough cloths or paper towels scratches through the thin coating layer.

## Probability

**Very low under normal conditions.** The panels are protected inside the wood frame (edges sealed from moisture), and the coating faces inward (away from direct contact). This failure mode requires years of UV exposure or improper cleaning. Indoor placement away from direct sunlight makes this a decade-plus concern.

## Repair Path

Same as [Acrylic Panel — Crack or Scratch](acrylic-crack-scratch.md) — panel replacement.

1. Unplug, open back panel, remove LED spacer.
2. Slide out the degraded panel.
3. Slide in a replacement panel (reflective coating facing the LED cavity).
4. Reassemble.

### Tools Required

- Phillips-head screwdriver

### Parts Required

- **1-way or 2-way mirror acrylic, 150mm × 150mm × 3.1mm** — same sourcing as [Crack or Scratch](acrylic-crack-scratch.md). ~$2.50–$5.00 per panel.

### Estimated Difficulty

Moderate.

### Estimated Time

20–30 minutes.

## When to Contact Support

- Same as crack/scratch — if you need pre-cut panels or aren't sure which panel is affected.

## Design Mitigations

- **Coating faces inward** — the reflective surface is on the interior of the cavity, protected from direct contact, cleaning, and UV exposure.
- **Wood frame edges** — frame channels shelter the panel edges from moisture ingress.
- **Care instructions** — packaging includes a note: clean the front surface with a soft microfiber cloth and water only. No ammonia, no alcohol, no paper towels.

### Preventive Care

- Place the mirror away from direct sunlight.
- Clean only with a soft microfiber cloth, lightly dampened with water.
- Never use glass cleaner, alcohol, or abrasive products on the mirror surface.
- Avoid bathroom placement unless the room has good ventilation.
