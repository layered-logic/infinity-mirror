# Frame — Physical Damage

The wood frame is chipped, cracked, warped, or otherwise physically damaged.

---

## Symptoms

- Visible chip, crack, or dent in the frame edge or corner.
- Frame no longer sits flat — warping from moisture exposure.
- A panel slot has widened or loosened, causing the acrylic to rattle or shift.
- Stain or finish has worn away, exposing bare wood.

## Likely Causes

- **Drop or impact** — corners and edges are vulnerable to point impacts.
- **Moisture exposure** — untreated wood absorbs water and warps. Bathroom placement or a spill can cause this.
- **Heat proximity** — placing the mirror near a heat source (radiator, heating vent) can dry and crack the wood.
- **Wear over time** — surface finish wears from handling, moving, or resting against rough surfaces.

## Probability

**Low.** The frame is solid cut lumber (2×4 stock) — structurally robust for its size. Minor cosmetic damage (nicks, dings) is more likely than structural failure. Warping requires sustained moisture exposure.

## Repair Path

Depends on the severity of the damage.

### Minor cosmetic damage (ding, nick, surface scratch):

1. Sand the affected area lightly with 220-grit sandpaper.
2. Apply matching stain or finish (if the frame was stained).
3. Seal with a light coat of polyurethane or wax.

No disassembly needed.

### Structural damage (crack, warp, broken panel slot):

1. Unplug and fully disassemble the mirror — back panel, LED spacer, acrylic panels, electronics.
2. Remove the damaged frame piece(s).
3. Cut a replacement from matching lumber (standard 2×4 stock, available at any hardware store).
4. Route or saw the panel slot channels to match the original frame profile.
5. Reassemble with the new frame piece(s).

### Tools Required

- 220-grit sandpaper (cosmetic repair)
- Phillips-head screwdriver (disassembly)
- Saw + router or table saw with dado blade (structural replacement — the panel channel is the tricky part)

### Parts Required

- **2×4 lumber** — standard dimensional lumber from any hardware store. Cost: ~$3–$5 for a full 8' board (enough for many frames).
- **Wood stain/finish** (if applicable) — match the original.

### Estimated Difficulty

Easy (cosmetic) to Advanced (structural — requires woodworking tools for the panel channel).

### Estimated Time

10 minutes (cosmetic touch-up) to 1–2 hours (structural replacement including cutting and routing).

## When to Contact Support

- If the frame is a custom or specialty wood that you can't match locally.
- If you don't have woodworking tools to cut the panel channels for a structural replacement.
- If you'd prefer a pre-cut replacement frame set shipped to you.

## Design Mitigations

- **Standard lumber** — no exotic or proprietary materials. Any hardware store stocks 2×4 in multiple wood species.
- **3-sided frame** — the open back means the frame is accessible and modifiable without destruction.
- **Screwed assembly** — frame is held together with screws, not glue. Individual sides can be replaced without rebuilding the whole frame.
