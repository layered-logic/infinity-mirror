# User Interview Outlines

Lightweight outlines for **20–25 min informal interviews**. Bill conducts impromptu — these capture the *data concepts* to leave with, not a script.

Two audiences:
1. **End-buyer** — prospective customer (Interior Curator priority, Vibe-Seeker secondary). Brand audience priorities from [brand-positioning §3](brand-positioning.md).
2. **Showcase partner** — local coffee shops / makerspaces / cafes that host artists. Sprint Plan Week 4 specifically called this out.

Goal isn't a research report — it's enough signal to (a) pressure-test [brand-positioning](brand-positioning.md) audience assumptions, (b) inform Week 5 Figma wireframes, and (c) validate the Week 7 service-blueprint custom-order flow.

---

## End-Buyer Interview (~22 min)

**Opener (1 min)** — "I'm working on an LED art product as part of a school venture. Not selling anything today, just trying to learn how people like you actually shop for stuff like this. Mind if I ask a bunch of questions?"

### 1. Their space + taste (3 min)
*What's the room this would live in look like? How do they decide what goes on a wall?*
- Walk me through the last piece of art or lighting you bought for your home.
- How do you know when something "fits" your space?

### 2. Shopping behavior (4 min)
*Where do they look, what signals quality, what's the price ceiling.*
- When you're looking for something like this, where do you actually go? (Etsy / Amazon / IG / gallery / craft fair / friend recommendation)
- What makes you trust a maker you've never heard of?
- What would you pay for a piece that fits, vs. one you have to talk yourself into?

### 3. Reaction to the product (5 min)
*Show 2–3 reference photos of the infinity mirror in-context. Don't pitch — just watch the reaction.*
- First-instinct reaction — what does this read as?
- Where in your home does it go? (Or: doesn't go?)
- Mental price tag before I tell you anything?

### 4. Customization appetite (3 min)
*Buy-as-shown vs commission. The bespoke flow is the venture's primary differentiator.*
- If you could pick the size, shape, or color theme — would you, or is that more decision than you want?
- What kind of customization would be worth paying more for?

### 5. Control + smart home (3 min)
*Validates the app/webapp/native parked decision and the button-interface design.*
- For a piece like this, would you want to control it from your phone, or do you just want a button?
- Smart home stuff — Google Home, Alexa, Apple — do you use any of that today?
- (If yes) Would it bug you if a new device didn't connect to it?

### 6. Values alignment (2 min)
*Does the repair / sustainability story land or feel like noise?*
- If I told you this is built to be repaired — wires can be replaced, LEDs swapped, frame fixed — does that change how you feel about it, or is that not a thing you think about?

### 7. Discovery + close (2 min)
- If a friend showed you this, where would you assume they got it?
- Is there anything I should've asked you that I didn't?

## Showcase Partner Interview — Coffee Shops / Cafes (~22 min)

**Opener (1 min)** — "I'm a UW student and I'm working on a small lighting/art venture. I'm not pitching anything today — I'm just trying to understand how spaces like yours decide who gets to put art up. Got 20 minutes?"

### 1. Current showcase practice (4 min)
*Do they have one? Cadence?*
- Do you currently rotate art or have featured makers?
- How often does it change? Who runs it?

### 2. How they pick artists (5 min)
*Sourcing channels and gatekeeping signals.*
- When someone new wants to show work here, how does that conversation usually start? (cold ask / referral / IG / they curate)
- What makes it a yes vs. a no?
- Have you ever had it go badly? What went wrong?

### 3. Economics (4 min)
*Commission, consignment, flat fee, free-with-handshake.*
- How does the money side usually work — do you take a cut, charge wall rent, or is it just goodwill?
- What's worked best for you?

### 4. Logistics (3 min)
*Install, power, ongoing requirements — relevant because this is a powered piece.*
- What do you require from the artist on the install side? Power needs ever come up?
- Anything that's a non-starter? (size limits, content, theme constraints)

### 5. Fit for an LED piece (4 min)
*Show 2–3 reference photos.*
- Honestly — would something like this work in your space, or is it the wrong vibe?
- What about the maker story / the brand would matter to you?

### 6. Close (1 min)
- Anyone else I should be talking to?

## Data we want out of every conversation

Not every question gets answered — but try to leave each interview with a read on:

| Concept | Why we care |
|---|---|
| **Mental price tag** before disclosure | Validates the $65–$200 BOM-derived pricing brackets |
| **Discovery channel** | Informs marketing spend (Etsy vs IG vs partner-led) |
| **Customization appetite** (binary: yes/no) | Sizes the bespoke vs catalog split |
| **App vs physical control** preference | Resolves the parked Apr 22 native/webapp/Flutter decision |
| **Smart-home usage** | Sizes the Pro vs Pro+ SKU split |
| **Values resonance** (repair/sustainability) | Validates whether [right-to-repair](right-to-repair-philosophy.md) is a marketing asset or compliance-only |
| **One unexpected thing** | The piece you didn't know to ask about |

After each interview, jot 5 sentences in a session log under [docs/session-logs/](session-logs/) — the freshest impressions before they fade. Synthesis comes after 3+ interviews per audience type.
