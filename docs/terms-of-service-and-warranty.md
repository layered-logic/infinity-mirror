## 3. Terms of Sale

### 3.1 Standard Orders

Standard orders are accepted when payment is completed. We will confirm the order, build the unit, and ship it within the handling time stated on the listing. If we cannot fulfill an order, we will notify you and issue a full refund.

### 3.2 Custom Orders

Custom orders follow a defined design process so that both sides know what has been agreed before manufacturing begins:

1. **Inquiry & concept.** You describe what you want. We reply with a concept and a price band.
2. **Design proof.** We produce a rendered design proof for your review.
3. **Revisions.** You may request revisions to the proof. Up to **three (3) revision rounds are included** at no charge. Further revisions are billed at a flat revision fee disclosed before the work is done. Every revision is logged.
4. **Final approval & deposit.** When you approve the design proof in writing, you pay a **50% deposit**. Approval locks the design specification; the unit enters the build queue.
5. **Manufacture.** We build the unit to the approved specification.
6. **Balance & shipment.** The remaining **50% balance is due at shipment** — invoiced once the unit is built and ready to ship. The unit ships once the balance is paid.

The deposit and cancellation rules for custom orders are set out in Section 6.2.

### 3.3 Pricing, Payment, and Taxes

## 5. Limited Warranty

Layered Logic warrants that the product will be free from defects in materials and workmanship under normal use for the coverage period below.

### 5.1 Coverage Period

**One (1) year from the ship date.** Where consumer-protection law in the buyer's jurisdiction mandates a longer or otherwise different minimum warranty period, that statutory period applies instead — see Section 9.

### 5.2 What Is Covered

- Manufacturing defects in the controller, power supply, wiring, enclosure, and frame.
- LED failures (dead pixels, strip failure, color degradation) not caused by buyer modification or misuse.
- Firmware defects — addressed by over-the-air update at no charge, for the life of the product, regardless of warranty period (see Section 7).

### 5.3 What Is Not Covered

- Damage from misuse, accidents, liquid exposure, or use outside the product's intended indoor consumer environment.
- Cosmetic wear that does not affect function.
- Damage caused by a repair or modification that was performed incorrectly. Note: **performing your own repair does not, by itself, void this warranty** — see Section 5.4.
- Products not purchased from Layered Logic or an authorized channel.

### 5.4 Your Right to Repair Does Not Void This Warranty

The product is designed to be opened and serviced with a standard Phillips screwdriver. Using that right — opening the enclosure, replacing a commodity part, reprinting an enclosure part from a Layered Logic STL file — **does not void your warranty.** This is consistent with our [Right-to-Repair Philosophy](right-to-repair-philosophy.md) and with federal warranty law.

The only exception is damage that a repair itself causes: if an incorrectly performed repair damages a component, that specific damage is not covered. The rest of the warranty remains in force. If you are unsure how to perform a repair, contact us first — see the [User Repair Guide](user-repair-guide.md).

### 5.5 Remedy Order: Repair First

When a warranty claim is valid, we honor it **by repair first, replacement second, refund third.** This order is deliberate: repair keeps the object you own in service and is the option most aligned with the values of the product. We will not use the remedy order to delay or deny a legitimate claim.

### 5.6 How to Make a Warranty Claim (RMA)

1. Contact us (Section 13) describing the problem. Most issues can be diagnosed by email against our [Failure Mode Inventory](../Failure_Modes/README.md).
2. We will identify the failure and the remedy. For user-repairable failures we may ship a spare part directly to you with instructions. For others we will issue a return authorization (RMA) and a return shipping label.
3. We complete the repair, replacement, or refund and return the unit to you.

Please obtain an RMA before returning a unit so we can track it correctly. Contacting us first is a process step, not a precondition for honoring the warranty.

### 5.7 Replacement Parts Availability

Layered Logic commits to maintaining replacement-part availability for a **minimum of 7 years from the product's ship date**, consistent with emerging right-to-repair legislation. In practice, most components are commodity parts available from many suppliers; the commitment is most meaningful for the PCB and controller module. Enclosure parts are available as STL files on request for the life of the product.

## 6. Returns & Refunds

### 6.1 Standard Orders

Standard orders may be returned within **30 days of delivery** for a refund, no questions asked. The product must be returned in resaleable condition. You may use the original packaging or request a replacement box from us. Refunds are issued to the original payment method after we receive and inspect the unit. Original shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error or a defective product.

### 6.2 Custom Orders

Custom orders are made to your specification and are handled differently:

- **Before final approval (Section 3.2 step 4):** you may cancel at no cost. No deposit has been collected at this stage; only design time is spent.
- **After final approval, before manufacturing begins:** the 50% deposit is **non-refundable**, as it secures your place in the build queue and reserves materials.
- **After manufacturing begins:** the order is non-refundable. The deposit covers materials and labor already committed.
- **Returns of completed custom units:** because a custom unit is built to your design, it generally cannot be resold. A custom unit with a non-proprietary design may be accepted as a return at our discretion. A custom unit carrying **proprietary or buyer-specific imagery** (a company logo, personal artwork, a name) cannot be resold; its return is limited to the warranty remedies in Section 5, not the standard return policy.

Nothing in this section limits your warranty rights in Section 5 — a defective custom unit is always covered by the warranty.

### 6.3 How to Return

Contact us (Section 13) to start a return. We will confirm eligibility and provide return instructions. Returns sent without prior contact may be delayed.
